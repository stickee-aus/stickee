# How Stickee Thinks

A short introduction. The full governing document is [`STICKEE_CHARTER.md`](STICKEE_CHARTER.md) — read this page first if you want the two-minute version.

Stickee exists because most Australians aren't disengaged from politics because they don't care — they're disengaged because the system is genuinely hard to follow: who decided what, who's responsible, what the alternatives were, whether it worked. Stickee's whole job is making that navigable, starting from the question an ordinary person actually has, not from an institutional org chart they're expected to already understand.

A few things follow from that, and they're binding, not aspirational:

- **Stickee doesn't tell Australians what to think.** It's a facilitator, not a political actor — it doesn't hold positions on parties, candidates, or policy outcomes.
- **Impartial in process, not indifferent to evidence.** Every side gets its strongest argument represented fairly. Where the evidence genuinely differs in strength, Stickee says so plainly rather than faking a balanced fight.
- **Uncertainty is information.** A confident wrong answer is worse than an honest "this is disputed" or "this isn't known yet."
- **Mistakes are inevitable. Concealing them is unacceptable.** Corrections are public, dated, and explained — never silently applied.
- **AI may assist; people remain accountable.** AI can draft, classify, and summarise. A human is responsible for what gets published, always.
- **Accountability follows the decision**, not just whoever's face is most recognisable.
- **Essential civic knowledge remains free**, and Stickee's Australian focus is civic — never an ethnicity, ancestry, religion, or surname test.

That's the shape of it. `STICKEE_CHARTER.md` is where each of these becomes an operational standard — how it applies to disagreement, forecasts, community discussion, AI publication rules, accountability records, and a 15-question decision test every contributor should be able to answer before shipping a feature. Read the Charter before product, editorial, AI, moderation, or civic-data work; this page is just the door.
