# Data licence: CC BY 4.0

Stickee has two licences:

| What | Licence |
|---|---|
| Code (everything in `src/`, `scripts/`, `supabase/`, config files) | [GNU AGPL-3.0-only](LICENSE) |
| Data and written content Stickee creates (datasets, source ledgers, methodology notes, verified figures, docs) | [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |

Full legal text of CC BY 4.0: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en>

## What this covers

CC BY 4.0 covers data and content that Stickee contributors create, for example:

- datasets and tables we compile (e.g. fuel excise rates and revenue over time)
- source ledgers and verification records
- methodology notes and documentation

## What this does NOT cover

Third-party data keeps its own licence. Examples:

- Australian Government data (ATO, Treasury Budget papers, AEC, APH): check each source's copyright page. Many use CC BY 4.0, but not all.
- They Vote For You / OpenAustralia data: follow their terms.
- Logos, party branding and photos of people: not covered. Do not assume reuse is allowed.

When you add third-party data, record its source URL and licence next to it (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#evidence-rules)).

## How to credit Stickee

You can share and adapt Stickee data, including commercially, if you give credit. Use this format:

> Source: Stickee contributors, https://github.com/stickee-aus/stickee, licensed CC BY 4.0. Changes: [say what you changed, or "none"].

If the figure came from a government source via Stickee, credit the original source too, for example:

> Source: ATO excise duty rates, compiled by Stickee contributors (CC BY 4.0).

## Contributions

By contributing data or written content, you agree it is released under CC BY 4.0. By contributing code, you agree it is released under AGPL-3.0-only.
